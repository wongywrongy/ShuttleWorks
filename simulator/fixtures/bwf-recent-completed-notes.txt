FIELD GUIDE
MS = men's singles; WS = women's singles; MD = men's doubles; WD = women's doubles; XD = mixed doubles.
A format such as 32MS/32WS/32MD/32WD/32XD means the tournament listed 32-entry draws in each of the five standard events.
Tournament timing is given as the official date range from the source table; the final day is used as the completion date for ordering.

BWF LEVELS
- Super 1000: top regular BWF World Tour level; highest regular-tour ranking points and prize-money tier.
- Super 750: elite regular BWF World Tour level below Super 1000.
- Super 500: mid-high BWF World Tour level with full international draws.
- Super 300: BWF World Tour level for major international circuit events.
- Super 100: BWF Tour Super 100 level; development/pathway events that still award World Tour ranking points.
